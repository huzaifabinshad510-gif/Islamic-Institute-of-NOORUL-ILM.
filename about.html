Who We Are

NOORUL ILM is an Islamic educational institute dedicated to providing knowledge about the Holy Quran, Hadith, Islamic history and Islamic values.

Our aim is to help students develop good character, honesty, kindness, discipline, peace and respect for others through Islamic education.

Our Mission

Our mission is to spread authentic Islamic knowledge and encourage students to develop good character, discipline and strong moral values.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a knowledgeable and responsible generation guided by Islamic teachings, peace, unity and humanity.
